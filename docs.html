01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Diner

A diner on an empty street that never closes. Every meme that ever left the front page ends up here sooner or later. They sit at the counter, they take a booth, they stand at the jukebox. Nobody orders and nobody leaves.

Everyone in here is green and looks like a character from a 2003 console game, because that is the year the place stopped updating. The site is the window you look through from the street.

PLACE
One diner, one street lamp, one neon sign, open 24h
PEOPLE
Wojak, Pepe, Troll, Doge and Gigachad, all green, all regulars
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad
SITE
One page, no framework, the whole screen is the diner at 3 AM with the neon sign, the menu, the receipt and the bell around it

02 · TONIGHTThe regulars

at the counter ├── WOJAK one coffee, same cup since 2011, still not finished ├── PEPE corner booth, slice of pie ordered in 2016, never touched ├── TROLL behind the counter in a paper hat, zero orders ever, ready anyway ├── DOGE on a stool, bowl empty, such wait └── GIGACHAD at the jukebox, one song, fourteen years, no complaints

04 · CONTROLSUsing the site

INTRO
Four frames walk you from the end of the street to the counter, about three seconds, SKIP bottom right
SOUND
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts the jukebox
REGULARS
Five polaroids pinned in the top right corner, one per regular. Click one to open it big
GALLERY
← → to move, ESC or CLOSE to close
CALL THE WAITER
The chrome bell in the bottom right corner. Press it as many times as you like, the line under it tells you who did not come
THE GUEST CHECK
Bottom left, green diner pad. Contract address, the buy button, X and this page. Total $0.00, stamped unpaid
THE MENU
The lightbox on the right lists tonight's regulars and what they have not touched

06 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The diner does not keep tabs, it never had a customer to keep one on.

MUSIC
One CC0 jazz track on the jukebox, plays after your first tap, the same button mutes it
REDUCED MOTION
If your system asks for it, the neon stops flickering and the strip stops scrolling
